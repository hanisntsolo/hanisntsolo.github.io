Welcome! This page helps you quickly find the right content based on your goal.

## Who this site is for

- Students and beginners exploring real-world tech.
- Developers who prefer practical, copy-paste friendly tutorials.
- DIY makers working with microcontrollers, LEDs, and home-lab setups.

Start with:

Start with:
1. ESP8266 / NodeMCU setup basics.

3. Ambilight and advanced integrations.
